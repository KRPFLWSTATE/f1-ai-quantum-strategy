# Design R — reduced boundary pilot (fallback only)

Design R may be selected **only** if Design F fails corrected admission before new outcomes open. Resource-only choice; not a favourable-result amendment.

## Registered panel

- Retain all 120/80/24 fresh parents
- One outcome-blind checkpoint per parent, balanced across eight families and SC/VSC
- All five budgets and three policy seeds for training/tuning and low-cost sensitivity
- 1,024 draws, matched K=4, all four family/depth candidates before tuning, 120-case/30-resample mechanism panel
- Calibration primary: 30 s, frozen classical comparator plus tuning-selected C0 and C1, 2,048 paired evaluation worlds per distinct selected plan/arrival/commitment key
- Non-primary budgets: 128 paired evaluation worlds; exploratory; do not enter primary q or Gate F precision
- q: finite-sample one-sided 95% order statistic over **24 fresh parent-block maxima** (the maximum at n=24)
- Eight offline references and 12 isolated-latency measurements
- Synthetic-noise panel excluded only by its predeclared validation gate

## Limitation (must remain explicit)

One checkpoint per parent is not the full dossier experiment and not a superiority study. It preserves the independent-block basis needed for q and Stage 7 sizing, unlike a 4/4/2 pseudo-study.

## Status

Prospective. Not executed unless admission selects R after F fails.
